# RADICAL RECOMMENDATION: Eliminate "Who We Are" Entirely
**Date:** 2026-02-16  
**Analysis:** Data-driven assessment for Vista homepage  
**Bottom line:** Kill the section. Here's why.

---

## 🔥 The Case for Complete Elimination

### Core Argument
**"Who we are" adds ZERO new information to Vista's homepage. It's redundant, friction-inducing filler that blocks conversions.**

---

## 📊 What the Data Says

### 1. **High-Converting Landing Pages Eliminate Excess**

**From Unbounce (analyzing 100,000+ landing pages):**
> "Reducing form fields from 11 to 4 increased conversions by 120%."  
> "Stripping navigation from your landing page doubles conversion rates."

**Pattern:** Removal of non-essential elements = conversion lift.

**edX case study (52.68% conversion rate):**
> "In our testing, shorter copy worked better than longer copy. **Either you want to learn Python, or you don't.**"

**Key insight:** Stop over-explaining. Trust visitors to decide quickly.

---

### 2. **"Too Much Information" is a Documented Conversion Blocker**

**From Landingi (Landing Page Testing Guide):**
> "Too much information may overwhelm your visitors and deter them from taking the desired action."

**From Stanford research on landing page optimization:**
> "Too Much Text" and "Asking for Too Much Information" listed as two of the **Seven Deadly Sins of Landing Page Design**.

**From ConversionLab case study:**
> "32.8% lift in engagement" from simplifying design and increasing whitespace.

**Pattern:** Every extra section creates cognitive load. Visitors scroll past value to get to action.

---

### 3. **Competitor Analysis: Research Agencies DON'T Use "About Us" Sections**

**Delphi Digital (blockchain research leader):**
- Homepage structure: Value prop → Why choose us → Plans (CTAs) → FAQ
- **NO "who we are" section**
- **NO "about us" fluff**
- Gets straight to: What they do → Why it matters → How to buy

**What they focus on instead:**
- "One report, total clarity"
- "Combined perspectives"
- "Get in Early"
- **All benefit-driven**, not identity-driven

**BCG, McKinsey (consulting giants):**
- Homepage: Problem → Solution → Industries → Insights → CTA
- **NO "about us" above the fold or mid-page**
- About page exists, but it's a SEPARATE destination, not homepage friction

**Pattern:** Top-performing research/consulting firms prioritize **value communication** over **self-description**.

## 🎯 Addressing Counter-Arguments

### "But don't people want to know who we are?"

**Response:** They already know from the hero.
- Hero says: "research hub for builders, investors, and explorers in blockchain and AI"
- That's the identity. Everything else is proof (articles) and offerings (services).

**From Unbounce research:**
> "Benefits-oriented messaging is one of the best ways to drive conversions."

People don't care WHO you are. They care:
1. Can you solve my problem? (Hero + Services answer this)
2. Have you done this before? (Latest articles answer this)
3. How do I work with you? (Footer CTAs answer this)

"Who we are" adds narrative, not value.

---

### "But competitors have about pages!"

**Response:** About PAGES exist. About SECTIONS on homepage don't.

**Difference:**
- **About page** = Separate destination for deep divers (team, history, mission)
- **About section on homepage** = Friction between value prop and conversion

**Delphi Digital has:**
- FAQ section with "Who is Delphi Digital?" (buried at bottom)
- NO mid-page "about us" interrupting the flow

**Vista can have:**
- `/about` page (already exists: `/landing2/about`)
- Link to it from footer (for those who want deep dive)
- NO homepage section interrupting conversion flow

---

### "What if we just move it instead of removing it?"

**Response:** Moving it doesn't solve the redundancy problem.

**The issue isn't PLACEMENT. The issue is EXISTENCE.**

Moving "who we are" to a different spot still means:
- ❌ Saying the same thing twice (hero already covers it)
- ❌ Adding scroll depth between value and action
- ❌ Diluting the focus (more sections = less clarity)

**Better question:** If we removed it entirely, what would we lose?

**Answer:** Nothing. Hero + Services + Articles already communicate:
- What Vista is (research hub)
- What Vista does (AI, marketing, protocol growth)
- What Vista has done (latest articles)

"Who we are" is a vestigial section. Cut it.

## 🚀 What to Do Instead

### Option 1: **Eliminate + Strengthen Footer (RECOMMENDED)**

**Do this:**
1. **Remove "who we are" section entirely**
2. **Keep footer minimal** (just CTAs + navigation)
3. **Strengthen hero subheading** if needed (it's already strong)

**New homepage flow:**
```
Hero → Latest → Newsletter → Services → Footer
```

**Why this wins:**
- Clean, linear narrative
- Every section has a job:
  - Hero: Hook + identity
  - Latest: Proof
  - Newsletter: Engagement
  - Services: Offerings
  - Footer: Action
- No redundancy, no filler

---

### Option 2: **Eliminate + Add Trust Signals to Footer**

**Do this:**
1. **Remove "who we are" section**
2. **Add client logos / testimonials to footer pre-section**

**Structure:**
```
[SERVICES SECTION]
↓
[PRE-FOOTER - Trust signals]
Trusted by [ApeChain] [Obol] [Arco] + testimonial quotes
↓
[FOOTER CTAs]
Ready to work together?
[Get insights] [Let's talk]
↓
[FOOTER NAVIGATION]
```

**Why this could work:**
- Replaces identity copy with social proof
- Social proof > self-description for conversions
- Follows Delphi pattern (testimonials before CTAs)

**Trade-off:** Requires getting testimonial quotes from clients.

---

### Option 3: **Eliminate + Expand Services Section**

**Do this:**
1. **Remove "who we are" section**
2. **Add a single-line tagline below "what we do" heading**

**Example:**
```
[SERVICES SECTION]

what we do

Research-backed solutions for blockchain and AI builders.

[Service cards...]
```

**Why this could work:**
- Gives identity a home without a dedicated section
- Keeps it benefit-oriented ("research-backed solutions")
- One line vs 37 words = massive reduction

**Still inferior to Option 1** (why add even one line if hero already covers it?)
