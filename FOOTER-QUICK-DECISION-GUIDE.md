# Vista Footer: Quick Decision Guide
**For Isaac - TL;DR Version**

---

## 🚨 Current Problem

Your merge puts identity copy **BEFORE** CTAs on dark background.

**Why this fails:**
- Users at footer want to **act**, not read
- Identity text is hard to read on dark background
- Violates B2B footer best practices (Figma, UXPin, Zapier all do it differently)

---

## ✅ Best Option: Proposal A (Pre-Footer Pattern)

**Do this:**
```
1. Light section: "who we are" (shortened to 20 words)
2. Dark section: CTAs only (bold, prominent)
3. Gray section: Footer navigation
```

**Why:**
- ✅ Follows industry standard (B2B SaaS pattern)
- ✅ Identity gets readable space
- ✅ CTAs get full attention
- ✅ Future-proof (can add trust signals later)

**Trade-off:** Adds back one section (more scroll)

---

## ⚡ Faster Option: Proposal B (CTA-First)

**Do this:**
```
1. Dark section:
   - CTAs first (prominent)
   - Visual divider (line/spacing)
   - "who we are" below (smaller, de-emphasized)
   - Footer navigation
```

**Why:**
- ✅ Keeps single section (less scroll)
- ✅ CTAs hit first
- ✅ Still provides identity context

**Trade-off:** Identity copy still on dark (readability compromise)

---

## ❌ Don't Keep Current

**Current order:**
1. Identity text (you read this first)
2. CTAs (buried below)

**Problem:** Conversion blockers should never come before conversion opportunities.

---

## 🎯 My Recommendation

**Go with Proposal A** - it's worth the extra section for:
- Better conversion rates (CTAs get clean spotlight)
- Better brand storytelling (identity gets proper space)
- Professional standard (how top B2B sites do it)

**If scroll is critical:** Use Proposal B + increase text contrast for identity copy.
